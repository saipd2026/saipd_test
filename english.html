Vision

  • "Because Syrian creativity is a treasure.. we protect it, develop it, and build our future together."

Mission

  • We work to spread intellectual property culture and empower it, contribute to protecting tangible and intangible heritage, develop creativity, support innovators and rights holders, and help economic and social development through Syrian expertise and digital transformation, including artificial intelligence.

Goals

  • 1. Spread intellectual property culture and promote its importance through awareness campaigns and cooperation with educational and academic institutions.
  • 2. Support social and economic development by keeping pace with AI and digital transformation and leveraging Syrian expertise inside and outside the country.
  • 3. Cooperate with relevant authorities on cultural heritage, traditional skills, and related knowledge registration.
  • 4. Provide information and support to protect and empower intellectual property holders.
  • 5. Build partnerships with peer organizations to raise Syria's standing in innovation and intellectual property indicators.
  • 6. Stimulate the knowledge and digital economy through IP applications, research support, technology transfer, and job creation.

Principles

Inclusiveness and partnership Transparency and integrity Respect and heritage protection Innovation and modernization Empowerment and capacity building Sustainable development
